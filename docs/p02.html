Correlation Matrix

I am the PSY211 TA, and one of my main responsibilities is grading research reports. I help my students use SPSS to analyse their hypotheses. In this portfolio, I will use R to create scales, and then I will create a correlation matrix among many of the personality variables, and then I will make a series of heatmaps and network plots to visualize relationships between scales, as well as relationships among items within a scale.
