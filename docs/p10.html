There was a promotional running from Feb - Apr 2018. We have data on: * How long someone has been part of the flight loyalty program * How much they have flown with the airline * Points accumulated and redeemed * Marital status, education, income, where the person lives, etc

Analysis Questions:

  • What impact did the campaign have on loyalty program memberships (gross / net)?
  • Was the campaign adoption more successful for certain demographics of loyalty members?
  • What impact did the campaign have on booked flights during the summer?

The campaign relates to a spike in enrollment

Dumbbell chart looking at 2017 monthly enrollments versus 2018. This one really magnifies the differences to make it seem like there were huge changes between 2017 and 2018, even after the campaign had ended .

There do not appear to be gender differences in enrollments

We could use this chart to say that enrollments are lower for more educated people (but I imagine that if you make enrollment count proportional to the number of people with that degree, we would see something different)

There was a big impact on booked flights over the summer

You’ll see that even though the campaign ended iin April 2018, we don’t see the effect of the campaign on bookings until May-ish. Between the gross enrollments and the activity plots, it appears that the campaign led people to enroll a lot more than usual between feb and april, and this led to a spike in bookings for the summer.
