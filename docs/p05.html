Survival Analysis

This is from my FYP workflow - I ran a survival analysis with some of the drug intiation data. I haven’t included the behavior-genetic component, it is just a plain old survival analysis. Nothing fancy but it works.

The first thing I did was look at the NLSY79 Drug Initiation Data. I use Any_Drug_Use (a 0/1 variable) as the status, and First_Try as the time variable, representing the age people were when they first initiated. I don’t have use any covariates/grouping variable.

From this analaysis, I get some interesting results, which are presented in the table below.The Time column is fairly intuitive. Under n.risk, see that this represents the number of people who haven’t tried drugs yet (and therefore are labeled “at risk”) for a given time. Under n.event, we are given the number of people who have already tried drugs. There were some people who said that they first tried drugs when they were 0. For my FYP modeling, these people are filtered out, but in this case I am just leaving them be - perhaps they were heroin babies, or maybe just teens who thought they were being funny.

The other thing to note is the “estimate” column. this represents the probability of resisting drugs at each year. For example, at age 16 (which happens to be the mean age at first use for the sample), there is a 47% chance a given person has not tried drugs yet.

Next, I wanted to see if the survival pattern varies by gen1 and gen2.

I merge drug use variables from Gen1 and Gen2 and make a new variable called Group. Unfortunately, to add confusion, I labeled gen1 as 2 and gen2 as 1.

These results show that Gen1 and Gen2 follow similar drug-initiation trajectories, though there are differences. Based on the hazard ratio, one of the groups is 23% more likely to be at risk at any given time, but I am having a hard time figuring out how the Surv function is picking a reference group.
